This is a plan for the AI lab and assessment for Injazedu; it contains about 10 projects, presented step by step or project by project.


## First What is injazedu.co?

1. It's a platform specializing in providing professional licensing courses for male and female teachers, specifically for Qiyas tests in Saudi Arabia.
2. It includes sections for STEP and IELTS tests.
3. The platform employs trainers on a commission basis to deliver the courses.
4. The courses offered in each specialization consist of live lectures via Zoom or recorded lectures, along with a course textbook and course tests.
5. Lectures, textbooks, and tests are only accessible after course registration.
6. Some general tests are available to everyone, including those who are not registered.
7. Recorded lectures are hosted on bunny.net.
8. There is a Moderation Team for WhatsApp, Telegram, TikTok, and an Admin dashboard.
9. Trainers have a dashboard for monitoring and creating tests, both general and course tests.
10. The Moderation Team sometimes assists trainers with their work.

---

## The purpose of the AI lab and assessment

1. We focus primarily on interaction.
2. We create tests, both general and course-specific, similar to Qiyas assessment tests.
3. Assist instructors in creating tests.
4. The Modrashin team can publish tests on Telegram automatically.
5. The main purpose is to encourage interaction between instructors and students and to encourage students to enroll in open courses.
6. Make any helpful type of automation that helps achieve Injazedu's goal and Injazedu's work
7. Use local AI models & agents.
